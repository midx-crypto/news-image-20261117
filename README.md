A Python program that writes a one-page illustrated front page summing up the world's news of Tuesday 17 November 2026: 42 verified stories from 6 continents, 12 hand-drawn illustrations, a numbered world map, and a dated masthead. Three illustrated leads (Kerch Bridge down; US strikes on Iran; Quad meets in Manila), nine illustrated story cards (Ebola, COP31, measles, Nvidia, Gaza, Mongolia, Serbia, Bangladesh, An-12 crash), and 30 one-line briefs. Every story was checked against reporting dated 17 November 2026; disputed claims are attributed.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
